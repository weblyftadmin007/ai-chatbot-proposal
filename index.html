AI Chat Assistant for Your Business

A proposal — what it will do for you, and what it costs

Small and mid businesses in India · Launch pricing for a first client

1 What this is

A small chat widget on your website. When a visitor asks something, the assistant replies immediately — 24/7, in their language, using only your business information.

It does not pretend to be human and it does not make things up. It answers from your services, prices, hours, process, and contact details. If it does not know, it says so and points them to a person or the next step.

In one line: It answers the same repetitive questions that eat up your phone, WhatsApp, and inbox time — so your team can focus on real enquiries.

2 What it will do for you

  • Answer repeated FAQs instantly — day or night.
  • Reply in the language the visitor uses.
  • Sound like your business, not a robot.
  • Guide interested visitors toward the next step you want — call, enquiry, or booking.
  • Hand off to a human when someone wants to talk to a person.
  • Stay accurate by learning from your own updated business info.

This works for any business with a website — designer, clinic, salon, gym, restaurant, contractor, studio, consultancy, shop, tutor, or local service business. The details change; the pattern does not: people keep asking the same things before they contact you.

3 How it works

  1. You add a two-line snippet to your site once. The chat button appears for visitors.
  2. We load your business info into the assistant’s knowledge — services, pricing, process, hours, contact, FAQs.
  3. Visitors ask questions; the assistant replies from your knowledge.
  4. You can see what people ask, so you learn what customers care about.
  5. When your business changes, we update the knowledge and the assistant changes with it.

4 What the experience looks like

Visitor: “Hi, what do you do?”

Assistant: “Hi — we can help with that. Tell me a bit about what you’re looking for and I’ll suggest the right option for your business.”

Visitor: “How much does it cost?”

Assistant: “It depends on what you need. We have a few options at different price points, and each one is tailored to the scope of the work. Do you want a quick overview of what's included at each level, or should we start with what you’re trying to achieve?”

What this does for you: The visitor got clear, accurate answers and a natural next step — without waiting for you to reply on WhatsApp or email.

9 Honest note

A chatbot is useful if your site gets repeated questions. If you get very little traffic or almost no questions, the ROI is smaller — there is less to automate.

What it will do: make the questions you do get cheaper and more consistent to handle. What it will not do: replace a good offer, a good website, or a real sales conversation for complex work.

Unsure? Start with Lite and upgrade later.
